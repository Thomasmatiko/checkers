# DRAFTS – Advanced Checkers

DRAFTS is a modern Flutter-based checkers/draughts game designed to provide a complete and enjoyable checkers experience on Android and other supported Flutter platforms.

The game combines classic checkers gameplay with AI opponents, multiple rule variants, game saving, statistics, game history, challenges, animations, sound effects, and a modern responsive interface.

---

## 🎮 Features

### ♟️ Checkers Gameplay

- Classic 8×8 checkers board
- Legal move detection
- Piece selection and movement
- Capturing pieces
- Multiple captures
- King promotion
- King movement
- Turn management
- Automatic game-over detection
- Draw detection
- Legal move highlighting
- Direction indicators for available moves

---

## 🤖 Artificial Intelligence

Play against an AI opponent with multiple difficulty levels:

- Easy
- Medium
- Hard
- Expert

The AI analyzes the current board position and selects moves according to the selected difficulty.

---

## 👥 Game Modes

### Player vs AI

Play against the computer.

### Player vs Player

Play a local two-player match on the same device.

---

## 🌍 Rule Variants

DRAFTS supports different checkers rule variants through the built-in rule management system.

Available variants include:

- East African
- International
- American / English

Each variant can provide different gameplay rules and move behavior.

---

## 💾 Game Saving

The game supports saving and loading your current match.

Players can:

- Save a game
- Load a previously saved game
- Automatically save games
- Resume an unfinished match

The game also includes automatic periodic saving during gameplay.

---

## 🤝 Save as Draw

A game can be manually ended and recorded as a draw when there is no winner or loser.

The draw result can be recorded in:

- Game history
- Statistics

The unfinished saved game is removed after the match is completed as a draw.

---

## 🏆 Statistics

DRAFTS keeps track of game statistics, including game results and gameplay information.

Statistics can be used to monitor your progress and performance over time.

---

## 📜 Game History

Completed games can be viewed in the Game History section.

History includes information such as:

- Game result
- Opponent
- Number of moves
- Date
- Time
- Win/loss/draw result

Players can also manage and clear their saved game history.

---

## 🧩 Challenges & Puzzles

DRAFTS includes a challenge/puzzle system designed to help players practice checkers positions and improve their gameplay skills.

---

## 🔊 Audio

The game includes audio support for gameplay events such as:

- Piece movement
- Captures
- King promotion
- Game start
- Winning
- Losing
- Buttons and interface actions

Sound and music can be enabled or disabled through the settings.
